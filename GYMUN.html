A Letter from the President

A Letter
from the
President

— President Siyul Kim

GYMUN has, for over two decades, served as one of Goyang Foreign Language High School’s most distinguished and enduring student organizations. Throughout its history, it has cultivated students equipped with intellectual rigor, global awareness, and a strong sense of responsibility.

GYMUN is more than a debate club; it is a forum where complex international issues are examined through structured research, principled argumentation, and strategic negotiation. As delegates representing assigned nations, members analyze policies, reconcile conflicting interests, and ultimately draft comprehensive resolutions. Through this process, they develop critical thinking, persuasive communication skills, and a deep respect for diverse perspectives.

The executive board remains committed to creating an environment where every member can actively contribute and grow. We emphasize not only excellence and professionalism, but also integrity, cooperation, and mutual respect.

GYMUN will continue to uphold its tradition of leadership and academic excellence, preparing students to become thoughtful and capable global citizens in an increasingly interconnected world. We look forward to welcoming passionate individuals who are ready to embrace this challenge and grow with us. Look Above, Be Above, Reach Above!

— President Siyul Kim
